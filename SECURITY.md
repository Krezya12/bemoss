# Security Policy

## Coordinated Disclosure

We do our best to keep BeMoss free of security vulnerabilities, but reality says otherwise from
time to time. If you find a security issue, we are eager and grateful to hear about it — and we
ask you to report it responsibly.

## How to report a security issue

Please do **not** open a public pull request or discuss the vulnerability publicly before we
have had a chance to fix it.

Report it privately:

- Message our Telegram channel <https://t.me/mikchaz> and ask for a private contact with the
  maintainers.

Please include:

- A description of the issue.
- Step-by-step instructions or a working proof of concept.
- The affected version/commit and your operating system.
- Your assessment of the impact (if you have one).

## Reporting conditions

To keep everyone safe, we ask reporters to conform to the following conditions:

- All communication and disclosure is coordinated; do not put users at risk.
- The proof of concept should use your own data where possible and limit its impact to the
  minimum necessary to demonstrate the issue.
- Do not attempt to access confidential data of other users, run denial-of-service attacks,
  or use automated attack tools against project infrastructure.
- No social engineering against maintainers or community members.

## What to expect

We will acknowledge your report as soon as possible, investigate it, and coordinate a fix and
a public disclosure timeline with you. Credit for responsible reports will be given in release
notes unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

There is no formal bug bounty program yet.
